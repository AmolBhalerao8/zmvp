# ZOL Advisory Board Demo

## 01 — Opening
Repair shops still run critical work across disconnected calendars, inspections, messages, and parts portals. ZOL brings that journey into one intelligent operating system. We will follow a vehicle from appointment through diagnosis, approval, repair, payment, and long-term customer care.

## 02 — Live Shop Dashboard
The day begins on ZOL's live dashboard. Managers immediately see appointments, vehicles in the shop, approvals, revenue, and technician workload. The repair-order pipeline replaces status meetings with a shared source of truth. Every number comes from the shop's Cloud SQL database, so the team acts on current operational data.

## 03 — Appointment and Check-In
Appointments connect the customer, vehicle, concern, time, and technician before arrival. At check-in, the advisor records mileage, fuel, damage, and notes. ZOL creates the repair order automatically, preserving one continuous record without re-entering information or losing the customer's original description.

## 04 — Repair Order, AI Diagnostics, and Inspection
The repair order is ZOL's central workspace, combining status, diagnostics, inspection, estimate, parts, conversation, and billing. For this Chevrolet Sonic, the technician entered check-engine codes, rough-idle symptoms, and fuel-trim observations. ZOL ranks possible causes and recommends an ordered test plan, always requiring technician verification. The digital inspection captures green, yellow, and red findings. Its customer summary uses only findings the technician entered and never invents vehicle problems.

## 05 — Estimate and Customer Approval
Verified recommendations become an itemized estimate with labor, parts, tax, and transparent demo pricing. The mobile customer portal shows the original concern, inspection, diagnostic context, recommended work, and total. An approval updates the estimate and repair order immediately. Declined work becomes a responsible CRM follow-up instead of disappearing into notes.

## 06 — Parts and Technician Execution
Approved work moves into execution. Parts are tracked from needed through ordered, received, and installed. The mobile-friendly technician board organizes jobs by owner, urgency, approval, and parts availability. Managers see bottlenecks without interrupting technicians, while technicians move repairs through quality check and ready for pickup.

## 07 — Invoice, Payment, and CRM
Completed work converts into an invoice without re-keying line items. ZOL supports Stripe test payments and a labeled demo provider for this MVP. Payment marks the invoice paid, closes the repair order, and records the transaction without card data. The CRM turns declined services, maintenance needs, and post-repair check-ins into follow-ups. AI drafts friendly outreach using only verified shop data.

## 08 — Shop Intelligence and Close
Finally, owners get a controlled AI shop assistant. Managers can ask about approvals, tomorrow's arrivals, delayed parts, or technician workload. Answers use safe, tenant-scoped functions rather than generated database queries. ZOL connects the customer-to-cash workflow today, with clean paths to production payments, messaging, storage, and supplier data: a foundation for a more efficient shop and a better-informed customer.
